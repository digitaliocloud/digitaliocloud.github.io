NINE Vega

A private, single-user personal operations assistant

NINE Vega is a private assistant operated by Petra Malá for her own personal use. It is not a public service and it is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Email briefings. It reads Petra's Gmail to prepare a short morning and afternoon summary of messages that need her attention, and to watch for replies in cases she is following.
  • Draft preparation. It can prepare unsent Gmail drafts for Petra to review. It sends a message only after Petra explicitly approves it.
  • Journey planning. It reads Petra's Google Calendar events (time, title, location, status) to estimate when she needs to leave for appointments. It never changes her calendar.

Google data

NINE Vega requests only these Google permissions: gmail.readonly, gmail.compose and calendar.events.readonly. How this data is used, stored and protected is described in the Privacy Policy.
